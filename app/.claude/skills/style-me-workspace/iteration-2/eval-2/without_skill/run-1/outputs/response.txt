Here's what works from your wardrobe for a casual indoor birthday party on Friday evening:

---

TOP PICK: Cream Polka Dot Midi Dress

This is your best bet for the occasion. The polka dot pattern brings a fun, festive energy that's perfect for a birthday party — it says "I made an effort" without looking overdressed. The smart-casual formality level hits exactly the right note for a friend's flat. Dress it up with earrings or a heel, or keep it relaxed with clean white trainers or flats.

---

SOLID ALTERNATIVE: Black Midi Dress

The black midi dress is your reliable, always-works option. It's all-season, smart-casual, and easy to personalise with accessories — a fun belt, statement earrings, or a colourful bag can make it feel festive. Good pick if you want something low-effort that still looks polished.

---

CASUAL BUT CUTE: Blue Straight-Leg Jeans + Pink Cape Blouse

If you'd rather be in trousers, pair the straight-leg jeans with the pink cape blouse. The cape silhouette gives the outfit some personality and the pink keeps it light and fun. This combination is comfortably casual but still put-together — ideal if you want to feel relaxed and move freely all evening.

---

SKIP FOR THIS ONE:
- Red Gown — formal level makes it too much for a casual flat party.
- Mustard T-Shirt on its own — fine for daytime, but a bit plain for a Friday evening out.

---

Bottom line: Go with the cream polka dot midi dress if you want to look effortlessly fun. The black midi dress if you want easy and chic. The jeans + pink blouse if you want to feel casual-comfortable.
