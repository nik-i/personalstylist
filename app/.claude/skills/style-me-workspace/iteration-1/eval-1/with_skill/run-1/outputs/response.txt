For a client presentation on a Thursday morning in the office, this is the combination that hits exactly the right note — confident and polished without veering into overdressed territory:

- **White shirt** (white) — a business-formality staple that reads clean and authoritative; all-season so the July heat won't fight you, and it photographs well if anyone's recording the session
- **Tailored trousers** (black) — the anchor of the look; black keeps it sharp and serious, and the tailored cut signals intention without needing a jacket to back it up
- **Loafers** (black) — business-appropriate footwear that matches the trouser color for a long, clean line; comfortable enough that you're not thinking about your feet while you're presenting

This outfit works because the white-and-black pairing is inherently crisp and commanding — the client's attention stays on what you're saying, not what you're wearing. That's exactly what you want.

Note: This suggestion is based on a sample wardrobe. I also found two other combinations if you'd like to see them — a cream silk blouse version of the same silhouette, and a more relaxed white shirt with indigo straight-leg jeans.
