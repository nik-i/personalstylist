For a friend's birthday party indoors on a July evening — casual, but you still want to show up looking like you meant it — here's what I'd pull together:

- **Cream silk blouse** — this is the piece that does the work. It reads smart-casual without veering into "overdressed for a flat party" territory, and silk has a nice way of catching the light in the evening. Effortful without looking like you tried too hard.
- **Indigo straight-leg jeans** — the perfect partner here. All-season, and the straight-leg silhouette keeps things sharp and intentional rather than just thrown-on casual. Indigo reads a touch dressier than faded blue, which is exactly right for an evening birthday.
- **White sneakers** — clean, easy, and they nail the casual-but-considered tone. No one needs heels at their friend's flat, and white sneakers with this combination say "I know what I'm doing."

The blouse is carrying the look — it's what tips this from "just jeans and a top" into something put-together. Keep it tucked or half-tucked for a bit of shape.

I also found two other combinations if you want to see them: one swaps the straight-leg jeans for beige linen shorts (very summer, still works for indoors), and another goes more relaxed with a black graphic tee in place of the blouse.
