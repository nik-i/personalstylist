Outfit Suggestion: Rooftop Dinner Date — Saturday, July 26 at 7pm (Outdoor)

---

THE SITUATION AT A GLANCE

Late July evening, mostly outdoor rooftop bar, dinner date. Expect temperatures in the low-to-mid 80s°F at 7pm, likely dropping to the mid-70s later in the night — with a rooftop breeze that can make it feel cooler than the actual temperature. You want to look effortlessly polished, stay comfortable in the heat, and have a light layer within reach.

---

THE LOOK

Foundation: A flowy midi dress or a sleek going-out top + tailored wide-leg trousers combo.

Option A — Dress (easiest, most date-appropriate):
- A slip dress or wrap dress in a lightweight fabric like silk, satin, or chiffon
- Colors that work beautifully at golden hour and into evening: warm terracotta, dusty rose, cobalt blue, deep sage, or a classic black
- Length: Midi (ankle-grazing) elongates without being too formal; mini is fine if you're comfortable
- Avoid anything too billowy/flowy up top — rooftop wind can be an unwelcome surprise

Option B — Separates:
- A fitted, tucked-in blouse or a strappy cami paired with wide-leg trousers or a sleek midi skirt
- Linen or a linen-blend for the trousers keeps you cool without looking casual
- A monochrome outfit (same tonal family top and bottom) reads as elevated and intentional

---

SHOES

- Block-heeled sandals or strappy heeled sandals: your best bet — stylish, date-appropriate, and stable on a rooftop surface
- A pointed-toe kitten heel mule also works well
- If heels aren't your thing: clean leather slides or a dressy loafer in a metallic or nude tone keep it chic without sacrificing comfort
- Avoid stilettos (thin heel tips can catch in rooftop grating) and flat rubber-soled sandals (too casual for a dinner date)

---

LAYER (IMPORTANT FOR OUTDOORS)

- A lightweight blazer in a relaxed fit — throws over your shoulders effortlessly and looks put-together
- A drapey wrap or oversized lightweight scarf in a complementary neutral works beautifully with a dress
- A fitted cardigan in cashmere or a fine knit if you run cold

Do not skip this. Rooftop bars almost always have a breeze by 7pm, and you'll thank yourself if the temperature dips after dinner.

---

ACCESSORIES

- Bag: A small structured clutch or a mini crossbody — rooftop dining means you're moving around, and a big tote is cumbersome
- Jewelry: Keep it simple but intentional — delicate gold or silver chains, small hoops, or a single statement earring; avoid anything too large that will blow around in the wind
- Skip a hat or fascinator unless it's secured — wind will be your enemy

---

HAIR & MAKEUP CONSIDERATION

Not outfit-specific, but worth noting: rooftop + wind = styling that can fight you. A low bun, sleek ponytail, or half-up style will hold better than a blowout. If you wear your hair down, a light hold product or braided detail near the face helps.

---

THE SUMMARY LOOK (quickest version)

Silk or chiffon wrap dress in a rich color + block-heeled sandal + dainty jewelry + lightweight blazer thrown over the shoulders + small clutch.

This reads as "I made an effort, but I look like this effortlessly" — exactly right for a rooftop dinner date.

Have a wonderful evening!
