Here's what works from your wardrobe for a casual indoor birthday party on Friday evening — fun, put together, no effort wasted.

---

TOP PICK: Black Graphic Tee + Indigo Straight-Leg Jeans + White Sneakers

This is the strongest combination for the vibe you're describing. Here's why it works:

- The black graphic tee reads "personality" without trying too hard — it's the fun element that stops the look from being plain.
- Indigo straight-leg jeans are the sweet spot between casual and polished. They're smarter than blue jeans and a cut above leggings, so you'll look considered without being overdressed at your friend's flat.
- White sneakers keep everything grounded and easy to move around in — no heels required indoors.

To lift it for Friday evening: swap the sneakers for a chunky loafer or a low block heel if you have one, throw on a gold or silver necklace (layered if you've got it), and tuck the tee in slightly at the front to shape the silhouette. A small shoulder bag or clutch finishes it nicely.

---

ALTERNATIVE 1: Black Graphic Tee + Beige Linen Shorts + White Sneakers

If you run warm or the flat tends to get stuffy with a crowd, this is the cooler option and it works in summer. The black-and-beige contrast is clean and the linen texture adds a bit of interest. The same evening-lift advice applies — add a necklace and make sure your sneakers are clean.

---

ALTERNATIVE 2: White Tank Top + Indigo Straight-Leg Jeans + White Sneakers

A crisper, more minimal take. The white-on-indigo pairing is timeless and looks effortless. This one benefits most from accessories — it's a blank canvas, so a bold earring or a colourful bag is what makes it feel "birthday party" rather than "Monday errands." A blazer thrown over the top (if you have one, even an oversized one) would also make this feel sharper for the evening.

---

WHAT TO AVOID

Skip anything too formal (blazer-and-trousers, dress that needs heels) — you'll be overdressed at a flat gathering and uncomfortable. Also skip anything too slouchy like oversized sweats, even if they're your favourite — they'll undercut the "put together" part of what you're going for.

---

FINAL RECOMMENDATION

Go with the top pick: black graphic tee, indigo straight-leg jeans, white sneakers. Add one or two accessories to give it an evening feel and you're done. You'll look like you put thought into it without looking like you tried too hard — which is exactly right for a friend's birthday at home.

Have a great time at the party!
