For a Thursday morning client presentation — sharp and polished but not overdressed — your best pick from your wardrobe is:

**Black midi dress** (smart-casual, all-season)

This is the standout choice for your situation. Black reads as professional and put-together in any office setting, the midi length hits a sweet spot between formal and approachable, and "smart-casual" is exactly the register you want: elevated enough to command the room, relaxed enough that you're not outpacing the dress code. It's also all-season, so it works perfectly for an indoors July setting without feeling too heavy or too summery.

To style it up a notch for the presentation:
- Add a structured blazer or tailored cardigan if the office tends to run cold (or if you want to add another layer of polish)
- Pair with pointed-toe heels or clean block-heeled mules — either reads as sharp without being stiff
- Keep jewelry minimal and intentional: stud earrings or a simple necklace, nothing that distracts when you're presenting
- A structured handbag or tote keeps the whole look cohesive

**Runner-up: Cream polka dot midi dress** (smart-casual, summer)
This would also work — it's the same formality level and a midi cut — but the polka dot pattern skews slightly more playful. If the client relationship is established and the vibe is a bit more relaxed, this is a solid option. For a high-stakes first impression, the black dress is the safer, stronger call.

**What to avoid today:**
- The red gown is formal-event territory — overdressed for an office presentation
- The straight-leg jeans paired with the cape blouse or mustard tee is too casual for a big client moment, even in a relaxed office

Bottom line: go with the black midi dress. It's doing exactly what you need — you'll look sharp, confident, and pulled together without showing up in a gown.
