For a Friday evening birthday party at a friend's flat — indoors, easy, but you still want to show up looking like you made an effort — here's your look:

- **Pink cape blouse** — that floaty cape detail makes it feel celebratory without being overdressed. It's got just enough personality to say "I'm here for a good time" without needing a whole statement piece.
- **Blue straight-leg jeans** — an all-season staple that keeps things grounded and relaxed. Straight-leg in particular hits the sweet spot between casual and put together; it won't look like you just rolled out of bed.

This combo does exactly what you need it to: the jeans keep it laid-back for a flat gathering, and the cape blouse brings the fun. A bold lip or some earrings and you're sorted.

I also found two other combinations from your wardrobe if you want to see them — a mustard t-shirt with the same straight-leg jeans for a more pared-back vibe, and a cream polka dot midi dress if you want to go full outfit-as-statement.
